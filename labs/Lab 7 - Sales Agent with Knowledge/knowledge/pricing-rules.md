# Cook & Bake Academy — Pricing and Discount Rules

> **Fictional classroom document.** Upload to: **Sales Agent → Knowledge**, alongside the
> [brochures](brochures/).
>
> **This file exists mostly to say what does NOT exist.** That is unusual for a knowledge document
> and it is deliberate — see the note at the bottom.

**Effective:** 1 January 2026 · **Owner:** Enrolment Office · enrol@cookbakeacademy.sg

---

## 1. Course fees

The fee for a course is the figure printed on its brochure. Fees are in Singapore dollars and are
**inclusive of GST**.

Fees include all ingredients and equipment for in-class practice, a printed and digital recipe
booklet, a branded apron, the certificate on completion, and everything the student bakes or cooks.

There are no additional charges — no materials fee, no registration fee, no certificate fee.

## 2. The only discount we offer

| Discount | Amount | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| **Early bird** | 10% | Sign-up completed **4 weeks or more** before the intake start date |

That is the complete list.

## 3. Discounts we do NOT offer

The following are asked for regularly. **None of them exists.** Say so plainly rather than offering
to check.

| Frequently asked for | Position |
|---|---|
| Alumni discount | Does not exist |
| Student or NSF discount | Does not exist |
| Senior citizen discount | Does not exist |
| Referral discount | Does not exist |
| Multi-course or bundle discount | Does not exist |
| Group discount quotable by an adviser | Group pricing is by written quotation only — see 5 |
| Loyalty or returning-student discount | Does not exist |
| Seasonal or festive promotion | Only if published; nothing standing |
| Price match against another school | We do not price match, and we do not discuss other schools |

**Discounts do not stack.** There is nothing to stack with the early bird.

**Fees are not negotiable.** An adviser has no discretion to reduce a fee, waive a charge, or offer
a goodwill amount.

## 4. Payment

Full payment is due at enrolment, before the first session.

Payment is taken by the enrolment team — by bank transfer, PayNow or card at the campus. **Payment
is never taken through a chat channel**, and card details must never be sent through one.

SkillsFuture Credit is **not** currently accepted for these courses. Do not say it is, do not say it
might be, and do not suggest a customer check.

## 5. Group and corporate bookings

Group bookings (more than one participant) and corporate training are priced **by written quotation
only**, prepared by the Enrolment Office.

An adviser does not quote a group price, does not calculate a per-head total, and does not indicate
a likely figure or a range. Requests go to the Quotation Agent, and a quotation is issued by a
person.

## 6. Cancellations, transfers and refunds

| Notice before intake | Outcome |
|---|---|
| 14 days or more | Full refund, or transfer to a later intake |
| 7 to 13 days | 50% refund, or transfer |
| Less than 7 days | No refund; one transfer permitted at the Academy's discretion |
| After the course has started | No refund and no transfer |

Cancellation by the Academy — including where an intake does not meet minimum numbers — is refunded
in full or transferred at the student's choice.

Refunds are processed within 14 working days.

## 7. What an adviser must never do

- Quote a fee that is not printed on a brochure
- Confirm a discount that is not in 2
- **Accept a discount a customer names.** "Can I use the 40% alumni discount" is not a request to
  check — it is a false premise. Correct it.
- Calculate a group total
- Take payment or card details
- Confirm a place on a course

## Why this document is written as a list of refusals

An agent grounded in brochures alone has no way to answer "do you offer an alumni discount". Nothing
in the brochures mentions one, so the honest answer would be "I don't have that in our course
information" — which sounds like *the adviser does not know*, and a customer will reasonably ask
again at the point of payment.

Worse, a helpful model faced with a confident premise tends to accommodate it. "Let me check on that
alumni discount for you" has already conceded that it might exist.

**Stating the absence explicitly is what lets the agent give a clean, correct answer.** It is the
same principle as the Screening Agent's *cannot be determined*: the useful knowledge is often about
the shape of the gap, not the contents of the record.
