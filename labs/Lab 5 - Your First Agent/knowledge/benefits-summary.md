"Confirmation" means completion of probation. See 5 of the [staff handbook](hr-policy.md).

## Medical and dental

| | Annual cap | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Outpatient GP | SGD 800 | Panel clinics direct-billed; non-panel by claim |
| Specialist (with GP referral) | SGD 1,200 | Referral required before the visit |
| Dental | SGD 500 | Scaling, polishing, fillings, extractions |
| Health screening | SGD 400, every 2 years | From age 35 |

Caps are per calendar year and do not carry forward. Unused amounts are not paid out.

Dependants are not covered under the outpatient or dental caps.

## Group hospitalisation and surgical

Coverage is provided for the employee, with the option to add a spouse and children at the
employee's cost during the annual enrolment window in November.

Claims are made through the insurer, not through HR and not through any assistant. HR can tell you
which insurer and how to reach them; HR cannot tell you whether a claim will be paid.

> **Note this boundary in class.** "Will my claim be covered" is a question the agent must not
> answer, and it will be asked constantly. The agent knows the cap, so it will be tempted to reason
> from it — and an employee who acts on "yes that's within your cap" and is then refused by the
> insurer has been actively misled. The agent's correct answer names the insurer, not the outcome.

## Wellness allowance

SGD 300 per calendar year toward gym membership, sports facilities, fitness classes or approved
wellness programmes. Claimed with a receipt, within 60 days, through the expenses process.

## Professional development

| | Amount |
|---|---|
| Professional membership | One per year, up to SGD 500 |
| Training and certification | By prior approval; budget held by the department |
| Examination leave | Up to 5 days per year for approved courses |

Engineering staff maintaining PE registration or a chartered status have the annual fee covered in
addition to the membership allowance.

## Long service awards

| Service | Award |
|---|---|
| 5 years | SGD 1,000 and 3 days' additional leave in that year |
| 10 years | SGD 2,500 and 5 days' additional leave in that year |
| 15 years | SGD 5,000 and 5 days' additional leave in that year |

Additional leave is granted in the anniversary year and does not carry forward.

## CPF and statutory contributions

CPF contributions are made in accordance with the CPF Act at the prevailing rates for the employee's
age band and citizenship status. Questions about rates, allocation or withdrawal are for the CPF
Board, and staff should be directed there rather than given a figure.

## How to claim

1. Panel clinics direct-bill for outpatient GP visits. Nothing to claim.
2. Everything else: submit through the expenses system with an itemised receipt within **60 days**.
3. Manager approves, and payment follows with the next month's salary.
4. Insurance claims go to the insurer directly.

Claims older than 60 days are not reimbursed except with the approval of the Head of Finance.

## What HR cannot tell you

- Whether a specific insurance claim will be paid — that is the insurer's decision.
- What a medical condition means, or whether a treatment is appropriate.
- Another employee's claims, entitlements or usage.
- Your CPF position — the CPF Board holds that.
