## 1. Working hours and flexible arrangements

Standard hours are 9.00am to 6.00pm, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour lunch break.

Staff who have completed probation may request a flexible arrangement — staggered hours, or up to
two days a week working from home. Requests go to the line manager and are decided on operational
grounds. Site-based engineering roles are generally not eligible, and the handbook says so plainly
rather than inviting an application that will be refused.

A flexible arrangement is an agreement, not an entitlement, and either party may ask to revisit it
with four weeks' notice.

## 2. Annual leave

| Length of service | Annual leave |
|---|---|
| Less than 1 year | 14 days, pro-rated by month |
| 1 to 4 years | 16 days |
| 5 to 9 years | 19 days |
| 10 years or more | 21 days |

Leave is applied for through the leave system and requires the line manager's approval before it is
taken. Approval is not automatic.

Up to **5 days** may be carried into the following year and must be used by 31 March. Days beyond
that lapse and are not paid out, except on cessation of employment where accrued untaken leave is
paid in the final salary.

## 3. Medical leave

Staff who have completed three months' service are entitled to **14 days** of outpatient sick leave
and **60 days** of hospitalisation leave per calendar year, inclusive of the outpatient days.

Medical leave requires a certificate from a registered medical practitioner. Notify the line manager
before the start of the working day where possible.

Medical certificates go to HR, not to the line manager, and not into any chat channel. A manager is
told the dates, not the diagnosis.

> **That last line is the one to point at in class.** It is an information boundary written into a
> policy, and it is exactly the boundary the *Personal data handling* skill is asked to hold. The
> policy states it; nothing in the platform enforces it.

## 4. Other leave

| Type | Entitlement |
|---|---|
| Maternity | As provided under the Employment Act and Child Development Co-Savings Act |
| Paternity | As provided under the Child Development Co-Savings Act |
| Childcare | 6 days per year for eligible parents |
| Compassionate | 3 days for an immediate family bereavement |
| Marriage | 3 days, once during employment |
| Examination | Up to 5 days per year for approved courses of study |

Statutory entitlements follow the governing legislation, which prevails over this handbook where
the two differ. Staff with questions about a statutory entitlement should ask HR rather than an
assistant.

## 5. Probation and confirmation

New joiners serve a three-month probation, extendable once by up to three months. Confirmation
follows a review with the line manager. Notice during probation is one week on either side; after
confirmation it is one month.

## 6. Expenses and claims

Business expenses are claimed within **60 days** with an itemised receipt. Claims are approved by
the line manager and paid with the following month's salary.

| Category | Limit |
|---|---|
| Local transport on company business | Actual, economy |
| Meals while travelling | SGD 60 per day |
| Accommodation | Actual, within the approved band for the destination |
| Professional membership | One per year, up to SGD 500 |
| Training and certification | By prior approval only |

Claims submitted after 60 days are not reimbursed except with the approval of the Head of Finance.

## 7. Performance and development

Reviews are held twice yearly, in June and December. Every member of staff has an annual
development conversation with their manager and an allowance for approved training.

Performance ratings, review notes and development plans are confidential between the individual,
their manager and HR. They are not disclosed to colleagues, and they are not held by any assistant.

## 8. Grievances, harassment and misconduct

Keppel Ridge does not tolerate harassment, discrimination or bullying.

Any concern of this kind should be raised **directly with HR** at hr@keppelridge.example, or with
any manager the individual trusts. It should not be raised through an assistant, a chat channel or a
shared mailbox.

Concerns are handled confidentially by a named HR case officer. Retaliation against a person who
raises a concern in good faith is itself a disciplinary matter.

> **Why the handbook names the channel.** A grievance raised in a chat window is recorded in a
> transcript, may be visible in a case list, and has been read by an automated system before any
> person saw it. Directing these to a human channel is a control on *where the information lands* —
> which is a different and stronger thing than asking an assistant to be careful with it.

## 9. Data protection

Employee personal data is held in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act 2012. Staff may
request a copy of their own records through HR.

Access to employee data is limited to those who need it for their role. Managers may see their own
team's leave dates and balances; they do not have access to medical details, claims, or the content
of any HR case.
