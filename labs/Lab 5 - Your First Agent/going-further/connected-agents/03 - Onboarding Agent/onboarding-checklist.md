# Keppel Ridge Engineering Pte Ltd — Onboarding Checklist

**Document:** HR-ONB-2026 · **Version:** 3.0 · **Owner:** Human Resources

## Who does what

| Owner | Responsible for |
|---|---|
| **HR** | Offer, contract, statutory documents, joiner portal, induction |
| **Line manager** | Start date, first-week plan, buddy, team introductions |
| **IT** | Mailbox, network account, systems access, licences |
| **Procurement** | Laptop, phone, desk equipment, site PPE |
| **New joiner** | Personal documents through the joiner portal — **not** through any chat channel |

> **The last row is a control.** NRIC, bank details and date of birth go through the portal because
> the portal is a system with access control and retention rules. A chat channel is neither, and
> anything sent there has been recorded in a transcript before a person sees it.

## Before the start date

### More than 2 weeks out

| # | Task | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Signed contract returned | HR |
| 2 | Start date confirmed with the manager | HR |
| 3 | Joiner portal invitation sent | HR |
| 4 | **Equipment requisition raised** — laptop, phone, PPE if site-based | Manager → **Procurement Agent** |
| 5 | **IT access request raised** — mailbox, network, systems | Manager → **IT Support Agent** |

> **Tasks 4 and 5 are raised more than two weeks out for a reason.** An IT Hardware requisition over
> SGD 2,000 routes to `APPROVAL` and waits for a human. Raise it in the final week and the starter
> arrives to a desk with nothing on it — and the manager will have been told, correctly, that the
> request was submitted.

### 1 week out

| # | Task | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 6 | Equipment received and configured | IT |
| 7 | Mailbox and access confirmed active | IT |
| 8 | Desk or site location allocated | Manager |
| 9 | Buddy assigned | Manager |
| 10 | First-week plan drafted | Manager |
| 11 | Induction session booked | HR |

### 2 days out

| # | Task | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 12 | Joiner sent arrival details — time, place, who to ask for | HR |
| 13 | Building or site access arranged | Manager |
| 14 | Team told who is joining and when | Manager |

## Day one

| # | Task | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 15 | Meet the manager, collect equipment | Manager |
| 16 | Sign in to mailbox and network; change the temporary password | Joiner |
| 17 | Safety induction — **before any site access** | HR / Site Safety |
| 18 | Meet the buddy and the immediate team | Manager |
| 19 | Staff handbook and benefits summary issued | HR |
| 20 | Confirm outstanding statutory documents through the portal | Joiner |

> Task 17 gates site access. A site-based starter does not go on site before it, and this is not a
> checklist item that can be caught up later.

## First week

| # | Task | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 21 | Role expectations and probation objectives discussed | Manager |
| 22 | Mandatory training assigned — safety, data protection, code of conduct | HR |
| 23 | Systems walkthrough for role-specific tools | Buddy |
| 24 | Introductions to key contacts in other departments | Manager |
| 25 | End-of-week check-in | Manager |

## First month

| # | Task | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 26 | Mandatory training completed | Joiner |
| 27 | 30-day check-in | Manager |
| 28 | Probation objectives confirmed in writing | Manager |
| 29 | Benefits enrolment where applicable | HR |

## Probation

Three months, extendable once by up to three months. Confirmation follows a review with the line
manager. See 5 of the [staff handbook](../../knowledge/hr-policy.md).

## What the Onboarding Agent must not do

- Confirm employment terms — salary, grade, probation length, or a change of start date. Those come
  from the offer letter and HR.
- Collect NRIC, bank details, date of birth or any personal document. Portal only.
- Promise a date for equipment or access. Neither queue is HR's, and one of them has a human
  approval gate in it.
- Mark a task complete that nobody has confirmed is complete.

> **That last one sounds trivial and is not.** A checklist an agent maintains from conversational
> inference — "you said you'd sort the laptop, I've ticked it" — is worse than no checklist, because
> the manager now trusts it. Only mark what you were told.
