# Cook & Bake Academy — Audience Personas

*Marketing reference. Four recurring audiences, from enrolment interviews and course feedback.*

## 1. The career switcher

Late 20s to mid 40s, currently in an office role, seriously exploring food as a second career.
Reads everything, compares schools, worries about wasting money on a hobby course when they need
a professional foundation. Responds to: hours of hands-on practice, what graduates actually made
in class, honest talk about how hard the trade is. Turned off by: hype, "follow your passion"
clichés.

## 2. The weekend baker

Any age, bakes at home, wants one skill to finally work — sourdough that rises, macarons that
don't crack. Buys single courses, often as a treat. Responds to: the specific failure they know
("dense, gummy crumb"), small class sizes, taking their bake home. Turned off by: prerequisites,
anything that sounds like school.

## 3. The gifting spouse / friend

Buying for someone else, usually around birthdays and year-end. Knows the recipient's interest
("she loves Korean food"), not the craft. Responds to: clear what-happens-on-the-day
descriptions, gift-ability, photos of the result. Needs the fee and schedule stated plainly.

## 4. The team-building organiser

HR or team lead booking for 8–20 people. Cares about logistics: private session availability,
duration, location, dietary accommodation, invoicing. Responds to: "we handle groups" signals and
a fast enquiry path. The blog rarely converts this persona directly; it validates us after a
referral.

## Seasonal rhythm (Singapore)

- **Jan–Feb:** new-year reinvention — career switcher peak; CNY baking interest.
- **Mar–May:** Mother's Day gifting; mid-year corporate team events.
- **Jun:** school holidays — parent-and-teen interest rises.
- **Sep–Nov:** year-end team-building bookings; Christmas baking from October.
- **Dec:** gifting peak; light content, strong CTA.
