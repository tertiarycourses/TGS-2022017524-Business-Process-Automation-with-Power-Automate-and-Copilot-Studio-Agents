## 1. Priorities and targets

| Priority | Definition | Response target | Resolution target |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P1** | A system is down, or many people cannot work | 15 minutes | 4 hours |
| **P2** | One person cannot work at all | 1 hour | 1 working day |
| **P3** | Degraded but working, or a workaround exists | 4 hours | 3 working days |
| **P4** | Request, question or minor issue | 1 working day | 5 working days |

**These are targets, not guarantees.** Quote them as targets. A colleague told "this will be fixed
in four hours" heard a promise.

**Priority is set against this table, not from how the request was phrased.** An agent that infers
priority from a caller's tone produces a queue sorted by who is most upset.

## 2. Support hours

Service desk: **8.30am to 6.30pm**, Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays.

P1 incidents are handled outside these hours through the on-call rota. Everything else waits, and
the agent should say so rather than implying overnight work.

## 3. Categories

| Category | Covers | Typical priority |
|---|---|---|
| **Password** | Sign-in, lockouts, MFA | P2 |
| **Software** | Applications, licences, installs | P3 / P4 |
| **Hardware** | Devices, peripherals, printers | P2 if unusable |
| **Network** | Connectivity, VPN, shared drives | P1 if site-wide |
| **Access** | Accounts, permissions, group membership | P4 |
| **Security** | Suspected compromise, phishing, data loss | **P1, always** |

**Anything not on this list requires approval** through the Access Request Agent — including free

Software is installed from the **company portal only**. Never from a vendor site, a download
aggregator, or a link someone was sent.

## 5. What the service desk does not do

- Personal devices, personal accounts, or personal software
- Anything requiring a colleague to have admin rights — colleagues do not have them
- Data recovery from a personal or unmanaged device
- Training on how to use an application (see the Learning portal)

## 6. Standard equipment

| Role | Standard issue |
|---|---|
| Office-based | Laptop, docking station, two monitors, keyboard, mouse, headset |
| Site-based | Rugged laptop, mobile phone, rugged case |
| Engineering | Workstation-class laptop, two monitors |

Replacement cycle is **4 years**, or sooner where a device fails and is beyond economic repair.

**A replacement is a purchase.** It goes through Procurement, and IT hardware over SGD 2,000 routes
to a human approver before anything is ordered. The service desk does not control that queue and
cannot give a date.

## 7. Security incidents

The following are **P1 and are escalated to a person immediately**, without troubleshooting:

- A phishing link clicked, or credentials entered on a suspicious page
- A device that may be compromised
- Ransomware, or files unexpectedly encrypted or renamed
- A lost or stolen device
- Unexpected access to an account, or a sign-in the colleague does not recognise
- Anyone contacting a colleague and asking for a password or an MFA code

**A device suspected of compromise must be disconnected from the network but NOT switched off.**
Powering it down destroys volatile evidence an investigation needs. Say this every time — it is
counter-intuitive, and the colleague's instinct is to shut it down.

**IT will never ask for a password or an MFA code.** Neither will any agent operating on IT's
behalf. A colleague who is asked for one is being attacked, whoever appears to be asking.
